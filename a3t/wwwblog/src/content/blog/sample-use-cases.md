Not every process needs an agent. These four tend to pay off first because the work is
repetitive, time-sensitive and already has clear rules.

## SecOps triage
Enrich an alert, check it against policy, isolate or escalate.

## Trading checks
Pre-trade checks with every decision recorded.

## RTB bidding
Decisions inside a tight latency budget.

## Integration
Move data between systems with approvals where they matter.
